What happened?

The simulated offer loads late and adds 60 pixels above the buttons. “Confirm order” moves into the exact spot previously occupied by “Cancel order”. Enable the fix and replay: reserving the same space from the start keeps the buttons still.

Images can cause the same problem. Set their width and height attributes, or reserve space with CSS aspect-ratio. Reserve space for ads and other dynamic content too.

Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) measures unexpected movement. Aim for a score of 0.1 or less. Record a reload in Chrome DevTools’ Performance panel to inspect the shifts. Shifts within 500 milliseconds of a click or keypress are excluded from CLS; this demo’s delayed offer arrives outside that window.
