## How to answer

Write your answers in the spaces below the questions. Support your answers with clear reasoning, concrete examples, and references where relevant. Distinguish between architectural principles, implementation choices, and personal preferences.

Review the complete workbook together before submitting.

Your work will be assessed on the quality of your reasoning and evidence, rather than agreement with a particular architectural position.
